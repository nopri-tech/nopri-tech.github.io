Privacy Policy

Nautical Dominoes · Effective 19 September 2026

Nautical Dominoes collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.

What the game saves

Your progress, your settings, and whether you have purchased the full game. It is kept on your device. Nopri LLC runs no servers for this game and receives none of it.

Apple's services

If you are signed in to iCloud, your progress is mirrored to your own private iCloud storage so your devices share one campaign. If you are signed in to Game Center, achievements you earn are recorded against your Apple Account; no scores are submitted and there are no leaderboards. The full game is a one-time App Store purchase, so payment details never reach the game or us.

All three are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, and none of them sends us personal information.

Children

The game suits all ages, and since it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the save on that device. The iCloud copy is yours and can be removed in iOS Settings under your name → iCloud. Game Center achievements are held by Apple and managed through your Apple Account.

Changes

Any revision will be posted here with a new effective date.
