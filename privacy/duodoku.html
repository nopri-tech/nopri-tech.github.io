Privacy Policy

Duodoku · Effective 6 November 2026

Duodoku has no ads, no tracking and no accounts, and Nopri LLC runs no servers for it. Nopri LLC collects no data from the game.

What the game saves

Your unlocked levels, any level you are partway through, your Arcade runs and your settings. It is kept on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, mirrored to your own private iCloud storage so your devices share one save. Nopri LLC receives none of it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your Arcade scores are sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboards, where other players can see them with your Game Center name. This is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can sign out or change your profile in iOS Settings → Game Center.

Purchase

Duodoku is a paid App Store app. Payment is handled by Apple, so payment details never reach the game or us.

Children

Duodoku is not directed at children under 13, and Nopri LLC does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the save on that device. The iCloud copy is yours and can be removed in iOS Settings under your name → iCloud.

Changes

Any revision will be posted here with a new effective date.
