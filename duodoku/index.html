Duodoku

Sudoku with a colorful twist. Solve the numbers, fit the color tiles, then merge the two grids.

Get it on the App Store

How it works

Every puzzle comes in two halves. On the left, a sudoku. On the right, a set of color tiles to fit together. Each color has to land on one of every number, so the two halves give each other clues.

When both are filled, merge them. On a foldable iPhone, just close it.

What's inside

  • 81 levels that grow from 3×3 all the way to 9×9
  • Arcade mode with endless puzzles, three difficulties, and Game Center leaderboards
  • A pencil for notes, a calming color theme, and progress that syncs through iCloud
  • No ads, no in-app purchases
