How to play

Open the game's Settings and tap How to Play to run the walkthrough again.

Progress on more than one device

Progress follows your Apple Account through iCloud, and the save always keeps the further of the two. If a device looks behind, check that it is signed in to iCloud, then reopen the game.

Leaderboards

Arcade scores post to Game Center when you are signed in. Tap the controller button on the home screen to see the leaderboard for your current star level.
