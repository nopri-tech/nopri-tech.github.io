Restoring a purchase

The full game is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account. On a new device, open the game's settings and tap Restore Purchases.

Progress on more than one device

Progress follows your Apple Account through iCloud, and the save always keeps the further of the two. If a device looks behind, check that it is signed in to iCloud, then reopen the game.

Achievements

Achievements are recorded through Game Center. If none appear, make sure Game Center is signed in.

Privacy

The game collects no personal information — see the privacy policy.
